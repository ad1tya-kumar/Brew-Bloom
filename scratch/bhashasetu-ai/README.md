# BhashaSetu AI (भाषासेतु AI)
> **"Every Child Learns Best in Their Mother Tongue"**  
> **Smart India Hackathon 2024 — Government of Jharkhand Track (Problem Statement #1628)**  
> **Theme:** EdTech / Digital India / Inclusive Education

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary
**BhashaSetu AI** is an AI-powered, offline-capable translation and vernacular pedagogy tool designed specifically for rural and tribal primary schools in Jharkhand. It bridges the critical language mismatch between state-appointed teachers (who speak Hindi/English) and primary school children (who speak indigenous tribal languages such as Santhali, Mundari, Ho, Kurukh, and Kharia).

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Bilingual UI Switcher**: Complete site UI switches between **English** and **Hindi (हिन्दी)** instantaneously.
2. **Offline-First Edge AI (0 kbps)**: Simulated 4-bit quantized IndicTrans2 and Whisper ASR edge models running locally on low-cost tablets with zero internet connectivity.
3. **Interactive Working Translator**:
   - Live bidirectional translation between Hindi, English, Bengali, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, and Jharkhand's tribal languages (**Santhali in Ol Chiki script**, **Mundari**, **Ho in Warang Chiti**, **Kurukh**, **Kharia**).
   - One-click classroom scenario presets (Morning Greeting & Roll Call, Math Addition, Hygiene & Mid-Day Meal, Folk Tale Introduction, Science Observation).
   - Microphone voice input simulation with animated soundwave visualizer.
   - Realistic multi-stage pipeline loading animation (Acoustic tokenization -> Transformer alignment -> Native synthesis).
   - Dual-script output: Native script (e.g. Ol Chiki glyphs) + Devanagari transliteration.
   - Teacher's Romanized Phonetic pronunciation guide (empowering non-tribal teachers to speak the mother tongue directly).
   - Multimodal contextual educational flashcard aid with relevant emoji pictograms.
   - Synthetic speech audio playback and copy to clipboard.
4. **How It Works & System Architecture**: 4-step classroom workflow and interactive 4-layer system architecture diagram.
5. **National Sovereign Tech Stack**: Built around IndicTrans2, Whisper Indic, TensorFlow Lite, Bhashini NLTM API, and PWA offline storage.
6. **Measurable Social Outcomes & Policy Alignment**: Real metrics mapped to **NEP 2020 §§4.11–4.13**, **UN SDG 4**, and **NIPUN Bharat**.
7. **Pitch Video Showcase & 6-Member Team Grid**: Tailored for hackathon judging rounds.
