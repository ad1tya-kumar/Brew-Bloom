The Challenge

The Silent Classroom Crisis in Rural India

When a teacher speaks only Hindi and the child speaks only Santhali or Ho, learning stops before it even begins.

⚠️ The Ground Reality in West Singhbhum & Dumka

Freshly appointed primary teachers in Jharkhand's tribal heartlands often arrive without speaking the local mother tongue of their students. In Grade 1 classrooms, children listen to unfamiliar state curricula in silence, unable to comprehend foundational phonics or basic numeracy. NEP 2020 mandates primary education in the mother tongue, but severe teacher shortages and language mismatches leave millions behind.

The Innovation

Empowering Rural Teachers With Edge AI Pedagogy

Architecture & Workflow

From Teacher's Voice to Student's Comprehension

Engineered specifically for low-connectivity, noisy rural primary classrooms.

1

1. Speech Capture & Noise Filtering

Teacher speaks into standard phone/tablet. On-device acoustic filters isolate classroom ambient noise.

2

2. Intelligent Network Sensing

Dynamic switch selects local TFLite neural model when offline, or cloud IndicTrans2 cluster when cellular signal exists.

3

3. Vernacular Pedagogical Engine

Translates syntax while preserving tribal grammatical nuances, honorifics, and local cultural idioms.

4

4. Multimodal Classroom Delivery

Produces clear synthetic native speech, Romanized phonetic guide for the teacher, and visual pictograms for young students.

Engineering Stack

Built on Sovereign Indic AI Technologies

Leveraging Indian open-source foundational AI, quantized for edge hardware.

🇮🇳 Translation Core

IndicTrans2

AI4Bharat's state-of-the-art transformer translation model fine-tuned for tribal and scheduled Indian languages.

🎙️ Speech Recognition

Whisper Indic ASR

Robust automatic speech recognition model adapted to rural Indian classroom accents and ambient background chatter.

⚡ Edge Inference

TensorFlow Lite (4-Bit)

Quantized neural network weights running on budget Android tablets with zero active cellular connectivity.

🏛️ National Mission

Bhashini NLTM API

Integration with Digital India's National Language Translation Mission for standardized Indic corpora and benchmarks.

📱 Frontend & PWA

React & PWA Runtime

High-performance Progressive Web App with Service Worker offline caching for persistent zero-network classroom usage.

💾 Local Cache

IndexedDB & OPFS

Client-side persistent database caching tribal phoneme dictionaries and visual flashcard vectors permanently on-device.

🔊 Acoustic Output

Web Speech & eSpeak-NG

Cross-platform synthetic voice synthesis engine delivering crisp, audible pronunciation in tribal mother tongues.

📚 Curriculum Sync

JCERT Lesson Adapter

Automated alignment layer synchronizing primary school textbooks with state Foundational Literacy and Numeracy goals.
